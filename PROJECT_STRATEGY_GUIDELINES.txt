PHASE 1: BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING & PROJECT SCOPING
================================================================================

1. Core Executive Objectives:
   - Quantify historical revenue growth, profit margins, and average order value (AOV).
   - Identify customer purchasing behavior, high-value customer tiers, and churn risks.
   - Detect operational bottlenecks in fulfillment, delivery SLAs, and regional shipping.
   - Assess payment gateway reliability, refund rates, and COD cancellation risks.
   - Determine product category profitability to eliminate negative-margin sales.

2. Primary Stakeholder Personas & Questions:
   - Chief Commercial Officer (CCO) / Executive Leadership:
     * What is our Month-over-Month (MoM) and Year-over-Year (YoY) revenue velocity?
     * What is the blended gross profit margin across categories?
   - Marketing & Growth Team:
     * Who are our VIP/Champion customers vs. At-Risk segments (RFM)?
     * What is the customer acquisition trend and repeat purchase rate?
   - Logistics & Supply Chain Operations:
     * Which states/cities experience the highest shipping delays?
     * Does delivery delay increase customer cancellation and return rates?
   - Product / Category Managers:
     * Which top 20% of products generate 80% of revenue (Pareto Principle)?
     * Which products have high return/refund rates or negative gross margins?
   - Finance & Payments Team:
     * What is the failure and refund rate across payment methods (UPI, Credit Card, COD)?
     * Does Cash on Delivery (COD) have an unsustainable cancellation rate?

PHASE 3: DATA PROFILING & QUALITY AUDIT (DIAGNOSING THE MESS)
================================================================================

Audit each dataset across the 5 dimensions of data quality: Completeness, 
Uniqueness, Validity, Consistency, and Accuracy.

1. customers.csv:
   - Gender Inconsistencies: Values include 'M', 'FEMALE', 'male', 'F', 'Unknown', null.
   - Age Outliers & Anomalies: Negative ages (e.g. -5) and extreme values (> 100).
   - Contact Format: Missing phone numbers, varied prefixes (+91, leading 0), invalid emails.
   - Geographic Mismatches: City/State combinations that do not align geographically.

2. orders.csv:
   - Quantity Anomalies: Negative quantities (-5) and zero quantities (0).
   - Price Outliers: Extreme dummy entries (e.g. 999999.0).
   - Order Status Variants: Inconsistent casing ('delivered', 'DELIVERED', 'Delivered', 'shipped', 'canceled', 'Cancelled').
   - Calculation Reconciliation: Verify if total_amount = (quantity * unit_price) - discount + tax_amount + shipping_fee.
   - Temporal Integrity: Delivery dates preceding order dates (impossible chronological anomalies).

3. payments.csv:
   - Missing Data: Empty payment methods and null payment dates.
   - Reconciliation Discrepancies: Transaction amount vs. order total amount mismatch.
   - Conflicting Statuses: Payment marked 'Failed' but order marked 'Delivered'.

4. products.csv:
   - Missing Brand Values: Null or empty brand entries.
   - Pricing Errors: Negative list prices (-100.0) and unit_cost exceeding list_price (negative gross margin).
   - Stock Quantities: Zero or negative stock levels.

5. sellers.csv:
   - Seller Ratings: Null values and out-of-range ratings.
   - Status Tracking: Mixed status flags ('Active', 'Banned', 'Inactive').
   - Geographic Validation: Discrepancies between seller cities and states.

Deliverable: Data Quality Audit Report documenting total null counts, duplicate 
keys, outlier distributions, and referential integrity violations.

PHASE 4: DATA CLEANING & DIMENSIONAL MODELING (ETL / STAR SCHEMA)
================================================================================

1. Data Cleaning Transformation Rules:
   - Categorical Standardization: Map gender to standardized categories ('Male', 'Female', 'Unknown').
     Map order statuses to standard enum ('Delivered', 'Shipped', 'Cancelled', 'Pending').
   - Outlier & Anomaly Handling:
     * Replace invalid customer ages (< 15 or > 100) with cohort median or label as null.
     * Flag negative quantities as return/cancellation transactions rather than net sales.
     * Replace dummy prices (e.g. 999999.0) with product list price or historic average.
   - Date Standardization & Feature Engineering:
     * Convert all timestamps to standard ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS).
     * Calculate transit time: delivery_date - order_date.
     * Calculate delivery delay vs estimated SLA.
   - Financial Metric Recalculation:
     * Computed Gross Revenue = quantity * unit_price.
     * Computed Net Revenue = (quantity * unit_price) - discount.
     * Computed Total Order Value = Net Revenue + tax_amount + shipping_fee.

2. Star Schema Dimensional Design:
   - Fact Tables:
     * Fact_Orders: Grain = 1 row per order item. Contains foreign keys (customer_id, 
       product_id, seller_id, order_date_key), metrics (quantity, unit_price, discount, 
       tax, shipping_fee, total_amount, profit).
     * Fact_Payments: Grain = 1 row per transaction attempt. Keys (payment_id, order_id, 
       payment_date_key), metrics (transaction_amount, payment_status, payment_method).
   - Dimension Tables:
     * Dim_Customers: customer_id, name, clean_gender, clean_age, age_group, state, city, segment.
     * Dim_Products: product_id, product_name, category, sub_category, brand, unit_cost, list_price, target_margin_pct.
     * Dim_Sellers: seller_id, seller_name, seller_city, seller_state, seller_rating, seller_status.
     * Dim_Date: date_key, full_date, year, quarter, month, month_name, week_of_year, day_of_week, is_weekend.

PHASE 5: EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS & DEEP-DIVE ANALYTICS
================================================================================

1. Revenue & Sales Velocity Analysis:
   - Monthly and quarterly revenue trends, GMV vs Net Revenue.
   - Peak purchasing days/hours (seasonal spikes, holiday sales).
   - Average Order Value (AOV) across categories.

2. Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis):
   - Recency: Days elapsed since customer's last order.
   - Frequency: Total count of completed orders per customer.
   - Monetary: Total net spend per customer.
   - Segmentation Matrix:
     * Champions / High-Value: Recent buyers, high frequency, top spenders.
     * Loyal Customers: Regular buyers with steady monetary contribution.
     * Potential Loyalists: Recent buyers with average frequency.
     * At-Risk / Hibernating: High past spend, but no purchase in 180+ days.
     * Lost Customers: Lowest recency, frequency, and spend.

3. Product & Inventory Profitability:
   - Gross Margin Analysis: (Net Revenue - (Quantity * Unit Cost)).
   - Pareto Analysis (80/20 Rule): Top revenue drivers vs bottom performers.
   - Loss-making SKUs: Products sold below cost due to excessive discounts.

4. Logistics & SLA Performance:
   - Order-to-Delivery lead time by destination state/city.
   - Correlation between delivery delays and order cancellations/poor ratings.
   - Performance ranking of top sellers by fulfillment speed and customer rating.

5. Payment Gateway Health & Risk:
   - Transaction success rates: Credit Card vs Debit Card vs UPI vs COD.
   - Refund rate and cancellation percentage comparison (COD vs Prepaid).

PHASE 7: ACTIONABLE BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

1. Mitigate Cash on Delivery (COD) Risk:
   - Introduce prepaid incentives (2-5% instant discount or cashback) to shift 
     customers from COD to UPI/Cards, lowering high cancellation and RTO rates.

2. Supply Chain & Fulfillment Optimization:
   - Establish regional micro-fulfillment centers in top-demand states with high delivery 
     lead times to reduce transit times and improve customer satisfaction ratings.

3. Customer Retention & Win-Back Campaigns:
   - Deploy automated re-engagement email and SMS workflows targeting the 'At-Risk' 
     RFM cohort before they transition to 'Lost' customers.

4. Margin Protection & Dynamic Discount Guardrails:
   - Enforce minimum pricing guardrails in the catalog to prevent automated promotions 
     from creating negative gross margin sales.
